THE LAST MILE
A trajectory that stays coherent.
LiDAR · Manifold · Motion

01 / The problem

Accurate at a point.
Unstable over time.

Scene-coordinate regression can localize individual LiDAR scans accurately. But independent frame-wise predictions may jump between nearby poses. A vehicle needs a trajectory that makes sense between frames, too.

BiLi brings geometric structure and learned motion into the localization process—without carrying the offline teacher into deployment.

FIGURE 01 Why the last mile matters: consistent localization along a road.

02 / The approach

Geometry teaches.
Motion remembers.

Three complementary components connect a single-scan pose estimate to a coherent trajectory.

01

Implicit Manifold Regularization

An offline neural signed-distance field represents scene geometry. Projecting predicted scene coordinates toward its zero-level surface gives the pose estimator a physically grounded geometric target.

IMR · geometric teacher
02

Mamba-driven Kinematic Distillation

A stateful motion prior learns the temporal continuity exposed by the offline teacher. It carries useful kinematic structure forward without requiring the reconstructed field at inference.

MDKD · motion prior
03

Dynamic Manifold Gating

An adaptive Cauchy-kernel gate balances the absolute LiDAR pose and the temporal prior on SE(3), suppressing implausible jumps while retaining evidence from the current scan.

DMG · adaptive fusion
